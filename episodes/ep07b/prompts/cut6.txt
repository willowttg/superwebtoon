- It must NOT look like a clean vector line, a fine ink pen, or a digital brush. If the line looks perfectly smooth and even, it is wrong.

- Fill stays flat and clean (no shading, no gradients), background stays pure white with no paper texture. Only the LINE carries the pencil texture.
