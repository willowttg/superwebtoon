Reference images are CHARACTER SHEETS (and, if present, a size lineup image which shows the characters' relative sizes only). Reproduce these exact character designs and this exact thin pencil line quality. Flat colors, no shading, simple emotion symbols. Cute, small, harmless mood.

LINE QUALITY (most important):
- Every line is drawn with a real soft graphite PENCIL (2B) on paper, then scanned: a THIN line, about half as thick as a marker line, dark warm gray (never pure black), with clearly VISIBLE graphite grain - slightly rough, fuzzy, uneven edges, tiny speckles of texture along the stroke, darker where the pencil pressed harder and lighter where it lifted.
- It must NOT look like a clean vector line, a fine ink pen, or a digital brush. If the line looks perfectly smooth and even, it is wrong.
- Still one continuous closed path per edge: no gaps, no double or sketchy overlapping strokes, no hatching, no smudges outside the line.
- The path itself wobbles gently like a hand-drawn line.
- Fill stays flat and clean (no shading, no gradients), background stays pure white with no paper texture. Only the LINE carries the pencil texture.
